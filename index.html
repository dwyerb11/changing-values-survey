Our final values

What should guide decisions?

These values frame decisions about protecting people and sharing essential resources across surviving communities.

01

Security

protecting people and the water supply from violence, radiation and further attacks.

02

Accountability

finding reliable evidence and holding people and institutions responsible for decisions that caused the harm caused by their decisions and failures.

03

Fair access

distributing water and shelter fairly. Decision making that is democratic across the surviving human communities is essential.
